# locallyfirst

**Privacy-first tools that run entirely in your browser.** No servers. No uploads. Your data never leaves your device.

Powered by WebGPU for local AI inference.

---

## Why local-first?

- **Privacy by design** — Your documents never leave your device
- **No accounts** — Just open and use
- **Works offline** — No internet required after initial load
- **Open source** — Audit the code yourself
